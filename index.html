Step 1

Add the recording

Upload the episode audio. Studio listens to it on this computer, writes down every word with its timing, and turns it into a captioned video. Your audio is never uploaded anywhere.

Preparing…

The first run downloads the speech model (about 80–250 MB depending on quality). After that it is cached in this browser.

Step 2

Check the words

Click a word to fix it, highlight it in yellow, or start a new screen from it. Your script is the reference for anything the model misheard.
